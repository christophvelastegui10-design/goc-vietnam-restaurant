Our Story

Where every bowl tells a story

Nestled in a cozy strip mall at Legacy Drive, Goc Vietnam Restaurant is a labor of love — a family-run kitchen bringing the honest, soul-warming flavors of Vietnamese home cooking to Plano, TX.

"Góc" means corner in Vietnamese — a humble word for a humble place. But our corners overflow with flavor. From our legendary crispy Banh Xeo packed with shrimp and calamari, to our slow-simmered broths, everything is made with intention and heart.
